Flooring Solutions for Every Home

As a certified Mohawk dealer, Flooring Maxx brings you two premium flooring lines — Revwood laminate and SolidTech vinyl plank — each engineered for beautiful, lasting performance. We help you compare them side by side during a relaxed, no-pressure in-home visit.

Revwood Laminate

Authentic wood look with remarkable durability. Revwood combines the warmth of hardwood with a tough, easy-care surface that stands up to daily life.

  • Authentic hardwood visuals with natural grain patterns
  • Scratch- and stain-resistant for active households
  • Easy click-lock installation with minimal subfloor prep
  • Simple sweeping and occasional damp-mopping care

Best for: Active homes, living areas, and homeowners who want the look of real wood without the upkeep.

SolidTech Vinyl Plank

Waterproof resilience meets family-friendly performance. SolidTech delivers stylish plank visuals with a rigid core that handles moisture, pets, and heavy foot traffic effortlessly.

  • 100% waterproof core — ideal for kitchens, baths, and basements
  • Rigid core construction resists dents and temperature shifts
  • Built-in acoustic backing for quieter rooms
  • Realistic wood and stone visuals with textured embossing

Best for: Moisture-prone areas, busy families with kids and pets, and whole-home installations.
