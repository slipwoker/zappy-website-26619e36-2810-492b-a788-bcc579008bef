Choose the Best Floor for Your Lifestyle & Home

Every Toledo home has unique needs—whether it's heavy foot traffic, curious pets, or moisture-prone basements. Flooring Maxx simplifies the decision with two premium material lines designed for real life, backed by our certified Mohawk expertise and 10+ years of installation experience.

Revwood Laminate

Experience the authentic look and texture of hardwood without the worry. Revwood laminate delivers realistic wood visuals, exceptional scratch resistance, and everyday durability that stands up to kids, pets, and busy households. It's the perfect flooring solution for active families seeking beauty and resilience.

  • Realistic wood grain textures
  • Superior scratch & stain resistance
  • Easy click-lock installation

SolidTech Vinyl Plank

Designed for spaces where moisture is a concern, SolidTech vinyl plank flooring offers complete water resistance, cushioned comfort underfoot, and practical maintenance for busy homeowners. Ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and basements, it combines everyday durability with a soft, quiet feel that laminate and tile can't match.

  • 100% waterproof construction
  • Comfortable & quiet underfoot
  • Simple routine maintenance
